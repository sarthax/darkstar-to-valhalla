## Lights (Riftworn Pyxis), up to 3 per colour
Blue +50% drop quantity (max 150%, Cobalt Cell); Red +50% quality (Rubicund Cell); Yellow +25% EXP (max 75%, Xanthous Cell); Green +25% cruor (max 75%, Jade Cell); White raises Atmacite/Periapt chance.
Cells: 3,000 cruor at Voidwatch Officer (Cobalt/Rubicund/Xanthous; Jade not sold by officer), or 2,000 (Jade/Cobalt/Rubicund) / 3,000 (Xanthous) CP/Imperial Standing/Allied Notes at Purveyor, or Brown Chest. Cells traded to Planar Rift, per player, reset when Pyxis opened; saved on wipe/timeout.

## Weaknesses
9 triggers chosen at start: 6 normal, 2 high, 1 extremely vulnerable; job abilities, weapon skills, spells. A hit triggers a replacement of the same type. Hits raise alignment for all combatants; alignment from triggers shows in Pyxis and resets at battle end. Some NMs are immune to certain triggers. 30-minute limit; Pyxis disappears after 3 minutes unchecked. Non-voidstone holders still get EXP/cruor.
Full per-job trigger tables are in the page text (Job Ability / Weapon Skill / Magic sections).
