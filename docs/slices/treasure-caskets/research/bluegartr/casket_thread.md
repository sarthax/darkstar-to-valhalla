Random Tip with Gold Boxes - since a lot of people suck at this...

Some times you'll get lucky with the hints:

1: Combination is between 25-46

2: First Digit is odd.

3: One of the digits is 1.

Others you just want to narrow it down as best you can.

If you find the possible combinations are: 31 33 35 37 39... and you have three guesses left, pick 33... if that's wrong and the number is lower - then obviously pick 31. If it's higher, pick 37 next... even if it's not 37... the hint will tell you which to pick next out of 35 and 39. Shocking how many people can't figure that out.

Assuming your get these hints:

Hint 1: The second digit is odd.

Hint 2: The second digit is either 4, 5 or 6.

15, 25, 35, 45, 55, 65, 75, 85, 95

If you only have two guesses left, it's kinda a crap shoot. If you have three guesses left, I'd pick 45 first. If it's lower, you can pick 25 and either win, or get the right answer on your last guess. If it's higher, anything besides 55 or 95 is a smart guess. 65 and 85, if wrong, have a 25% chance to give you a 100% answer... 75, if wrong, gives you a 50/50 chance. 

You could take another hint and hope for "The first digit is 3, 4 or 5" - but there's a good chance you'll get hosed with "One of the digits is 5" - something you already know.
